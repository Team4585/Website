Robotics beyond competition

How we share engineering with our community

Students plan the lessons, carry the robot, answer the questions, and clean everything up afterward. Teaching other people is part of how we learn.

2025 outreach totals

230+students reached through school visits
40summer camp sessions across seven years
~200children reached through hospital STEM lessons
20children in Palmer Court FIRST LEGO League

Current work

Programs students run and support

Some projects happen every year. Others belong to a specific moment in the team’s history, so those are labeled with dates.

Current · Hospital outreach

Primary Children’s Hospital

Through Seacrest Studios, we lead engineering activities for children who cannot come to our lab. In 2025, students taught 14 lessons and reached about 200 children.

Current · School visits

Robots in local classrooms

We bring robots and small engineering challenges to nearby schools. More than 230 students took part in visits counted in the 2025 Impact presentation.

Since 2013 · FIRST LEGO League

Mentoring and event support

Team members mentor younger teams, build mission models, and volunteer at qualifiers and state events.

Since 2017 · Summer camps

Student-taught robotics camps

Elementary and middle school students build and program robots using lessons written and taught by Husky Robotics students. Camp revenue helps fund the team.

2020–2021 archive · Palmer Court

Two FIRST LEGO League teams

Students helped start and mentor an Explore team and a Challenge team for children at Palmer Court.

2015 archive · Assistive technology

iTRAC

Students worked with the Opportunity Foundation of America on a prototype intended to help children with limited communication and motor skills learn and communicate.
