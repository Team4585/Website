Serritor

Serritor uses WCP Swerve XS modules, an over-the-bumper intake, a triple-ball shooter, and an extendable hopper. It competed at the Idaho and Utah regionals.

Serritor came together through quick prototypes, CAD reviews, late wiring changes, and repeated testing. The first version of a mechanism rarely survives unchanged. Students learn to keep the useful part of an idea, fix the part that failed, and get the robot running again before the next match.

The 2026 team brought about 15 new students into the program. That meant the build was also a teaching project: experienced students had to explain why a part was shaped a certain way, how to crimp a connector, and what the code expected the mechanism to do.
