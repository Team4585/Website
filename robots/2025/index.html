Riptide

Riptide used a two-stage continuous elevator to lift coral and algae. It competed at the Utah Regional and the FIRST Championship in Houston.

See official results ↗
Lift
Two-stage continuous elevator
Game pieces
Coral and algae
Championship
Archimedes Division

Riptide’s season became about more than its mechanisms. At the Utah Regional, the team received the FIRST Impact Award for its community work and qualified for the FIRST Championship.

Students brought Riptide to Houston while the outreach and awards team presented the same programs described across this site. Sponsors covered the travel costs, so students did not pay to make the trip.
