2024 · CRESCENDO · Archive

Johann Sebastian Barque

The 2024 robot competed in CRESCENDO at the Utah Regional, where Husky Robotics received the Team Spirit Award.

See official results ↗

CRESCENDO asked robots to collect foam rings called notes, score them in field goals, and climb at the end of a match. The official FIRST record lists the team’s robot as Johann Sebastian Barque.

The team also received the Team Spirit Award at the Utah Regional. The season is included here to connect the older Weebly archive to the current robot records.
