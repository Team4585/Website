A place to learn by making something real

You do not need to arrive knowing how to build a robot. New students learn beside people who were new last year, and everyone eventually has to explain their work to someone else.

Why the team exists

Build a permanent and visible presence in our community.

Husky Robotics gives students room to practice engineering, programming, business, communication, and leadership on projects with real deadlines and real consequences.

Sometimes a mechanism fails. Sometimes the wiring is wrong. Sometimes the presentation needs another rewrite. Students learn to find the problem, ask for help, and try again.

How the work is divided

There is more than one way to contribute

01

Engineering

Sketch ideas, test cardboard and scrap prototypes, build the robot in CAD, machine parts, wire electronics, and write the code that makes everything move.

02

Operations

Organize outreach, summer camps, grants, fundraising, awards, media, and the practical work that keeps the team running.

03

Performance

Study the game, choose a strategy, organize the drive team, scout other robots, and make sure the machine is ready to do its job on the field.

After school at Hillcrest

For families

What students need

  • Curiosity and a willingness to work with other people
  • A FIRST account and current consent forms before competition
  • Communication with the mentor about schedules and travel
  • No membership fees and no team travel fees.

Registration steps can change. The mentor will send families the current forms and deadlines.
