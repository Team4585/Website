Two ways into the work

Join the team or help keep it free.

Students bring time and curiosity. Sponsors provide registration, travel, robot parts, tools, uniforms, and the community programs we run all year.

Students

You can start without experience.

Learn CAD, machining, wiring, programming, strategy, outreach, fundraising, media, or whatever job the team discovers next.

  • When: Monday through Thursday after school until 6 p.m.
  • Where: Hillcrest High School
  • Attendance: Flexible; come when you can
  • Cost: No membership fees and no team travel fees.
