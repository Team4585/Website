Hillcrest High School · Midvale, Utah Student-built since 2013

Two ways into the work

Join the team or help keep it free.

Students bring their time. Sponsors, grants, and summer camps fund our $45,000 annual plan for registration, travel, robot parts, uniforms, and outreach. Students pay no membership or team travel fees.

Students

Join our 35-student team.

You can start without experience and learn CAD in Onshape, machining, wiring, programming, scouting, strategy, outreach, fundraising, or media.

  • When: Monday through Thursday after school until 6 p.m.
  • Where: Hillcrest High School
  • Attendance: Flexible; come when you can
  • Cost: No membership fees and no team travel fees.

2026–2027 plan

Where $45,000 goes

We’re planning for two regionals in 2027: the Intermountain West Regional (March 24–27) and the Las Vegas Regional (March 31–April 3), plus the FIRST Championship (April 28–May 1) if we qualify. Actual costs and attendance depend on registration and travel arrangements.
