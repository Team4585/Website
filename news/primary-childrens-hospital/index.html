Community · 2025 program

Engineering lessons at Primary Children’s Hospital

Through Seacrest Studios, students led 14 hands-on engineering lessons and reached about 200 children during 2025.

Not every child can visit our lab or attend a robotics camp. Through Seacrest Studios at Primary Children’s Hospital, we bring small engineering projects to them.

In 2025, team members taught 14 lessons and reached about 200 children. The activities are built to work in a hospital setting, so they have to be clear, hands-on, and flexible. Students learn quickly that teaching a project is different from building it themselves. You have to explain each step, notice when someone is stuck, and change the plan when the room needs something else.

These visits are part of a larger outreach program that includes school demonstrations, summer camps, FIRST LEGO League mentoring, and volunteer work at Utah robotics events.
