Community · 2025 program

Engineering lessons at Primary Children’s Hospital

Through Seacrest Studios, students taught 14 engineering lessons and reached about 200 children in 2025.

Not every child can come to our lab or a camp, so we go to them. On Friday afternoons we bring a small engineering project to Seacrest Studios at Primary Children’s Hospital.

In 2025, team members taught 14 lessons and reached about 200 children. The activities are built to work in a hospital setting, so they have to be quick to set up and easy to change on the spot. Teaching a project is harder than building it: you have to explain each step, notice when someone is stuck, and change the plan when the room needs something else.

We also visit schools (more than 230 students in 2025), run summer camps (40 sessions over seven years), mentor FIRST LEGO League teams, and volunteer at Utah robotics events.
