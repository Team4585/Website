Team news · January 10, 2026

Utah teams started the 2026 season at Hillcrest

We hosted the 2026 FRC Kickoff so local teams could watch the game reveal, compare notes, and begin the season in the same room.

Kickoff is the first day anyone sees the new FIRST Robotics Competition game. On January 10, teams from around Utah came to Hillcrest to watch the reveal and start reading the rules together.

The first few hours of a season are loud and a little chaotic. People sketch mechanisms on whiteboards, measure game pieces, argue about strategy, and build rough prototypes out of whatever is close by. Hosting the event lets teams trade ideas before everyone disappears into their own shops for build season.

It also gives our newer students a useful first look at the larger FIRST community. The team is bigger this year, and many of our 35 students were seeing an FRC season begin for the first time.
