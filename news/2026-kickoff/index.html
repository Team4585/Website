Team news · January 10, 2026

Utah teams started the 2026 season at Hillcrest

Utah teams came to Hillcrest to watch the REBUILT game reveal and start reading the rules together.

Kickoff is the first day anyone sees the new FIRST Robotics Competition game. In 2026 that game was REBUILT. On January 10, teams from around Utah came to Hillcrest to watch the reveal and start reading the rules together.

The first thing we do after kickoff is strategy: deciding which parts of the game are worth chasing. Hosting meant doing that with other teams in the building, trading ideas before everyone went back to their own shops for build season.

For many of our 20 students, it was the first FRC season they had seen begin, and they saw it alongside other Utah teams instead of only our own.
