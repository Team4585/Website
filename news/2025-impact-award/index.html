Team milestone · March 15, 2025

Our outreach work took us to Worlds in 2025

The FIRST Impact Award at the Utah Regional recognized years of work in schools, hospitals, summer camps, and the Utah FIRST community.

At the 2025 Utah Regional, Husky Robotics received the Regional FIRST Impact Award. The award qualified us for the FIRST Championship in Houston, where Riptide competed in the Archimedes Division.

The judges looked at work that stretches across many seasons. We have run robotics camps since 2017, mentored FIRST LEGO League teams, volunteered at events, visited local schools, and brought engineering lessons to children at Primary Children’s Hospital. Students wrote the award submission and presentation, gathered the numbers, practiced the interview, and explained what the work has meant to the team.

Going to Houston was expensive, but students did not pay travel fees. Sponsors, grants, and the camps we run covered the trip. That is the same model we use for every team member: if someone wants to learn and is willing to work, cost should not keep them out of the lab.
