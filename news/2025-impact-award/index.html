Team milestone · March 15, 2025

The first Utah team to win Impact twice

Our second time winning FIRST’s top team award. In 2021 it was still called the Chairman’s Award. This one sent Riptide to the FIRST Championship in Houston.

At the 2025 Utah Regional, Husky Robotics received the Regional FIRST Impact Award. The award qualified us for the FIRST Championship in Houston, where Riptide competed in the Archimedes Division. We also won it in 2021, when it was still called the Regional Chairman’s Award, which made us the first Utah team to win it twice.

The judges looked at work going back to 2013. We have run robotics camps since 2017, mentored FIRST LEGO League teams, volunteered at events, visited local schools, and brought engineering lessons to children at Primary Children’s Hospital. Students wrote the award submission and presentation, gathered the numbers, and practiced the interview.

Going to Houston was expensive, but students did not pay travel fees. Sponsors, grants, and the camps we run covered the trip. It works that way for every student and every trip, so money is never the reason someone can’t join.
