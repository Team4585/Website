Students and families

You can join before you know how any of this works.

You can learn CAD, machining, wiring, programming, scouting, strategy, outreach, or fundraising. We get as many students certified on the shop machines as we can. Attendance is flexible, and there are no membership or travel fees.

How to join

Keep the team free

Nobody on this team pays to join or to travel.

Sponsors, grants, and the summer camps we run cover it. Our $45,000 plan for 2026–2027 covers two regionals, the FIRST Championship if we qualify, robot parts, and outreach all year.
