2026 · REBUILT

Serritor

Serritor uses WCP Swerve XS modules, an over-the-bumper intake, a triple-ball shooter, and an extendable hopper. It competed at the Idaho and Utah regionals.

2025 · REEFSCAPE

Riptide

Riptide used a two-stage continuous elevator to lift coral and algae. It competed at the Utah Regional and the FIRST Championship in Houston.

2024 · CRESCENDO

Johann Sebastian Barque

The 2024 robot competed in CRESCENDO at the Utah Regional, where Husky Robotics received the Team Spirit Award.

2023 · CHARGED UP

BuCkI

BuCkI competed at the Utah Regional, where the team captained the sixth playoff alliance and received the Gracious Professionalism Award.

Students and families

You can join before you know how any of this works.

Students learn CAD, machining, wiring, programming, strategy, outreach, fundraising, and the practical work that keeps a team running. Attendance is flexible. No membership fees and no team travel fees.
